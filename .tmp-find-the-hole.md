📘 Find the Hole, Fill the Hole™ Playbook

Unlocking Underserved Goldmines in Any Market

🔷 What This Is

This playbook is your personal system for spotting gaps in markets, products, or services—and turning those gaps into scalable, profitable business opportunities. The foundation is simple: wherever there's frustration, friction, or unmet demand, there's opportunity.

🔷 Core Framework: Find the Hole, Fill the Hole™

This framework helps you consistently identify market gaps and build offers that dominate:1️⃣ Observe Friction or Frustration   • What do people hate doing?   • What’s inefficient, overpriced, or broken?2️⃣ Spot Gaps in Value or Experience   • What’s missing, clunky, outdated?   • Where’s the experience poor or unoptimized?3️⃣ Quantify the Demand   • Are people already spending money in this space?   • Are others succeeding with a weaker offer?4️⃣ Build the Plug   • Deliver a better, faster, cheaper, or simplified solution.   • Use the hybrid model (Product + Service) if it gives a competitive edge.5️⃣ Test and Sell Fast   • Talk to users. Pre-sell it. Launch the MVP. Iterate fast.

🔷 Tool: The Underserved Goldmine™

Use these high-leverage questions to find hidden pain points:• What sucks about this?• What’s missing?• Where do people settle for 'just okay'?• What would a 10x version of this look like?• What would make this experience effortless?This tool reveals overlooked frustrations. Fix them, and you own the market.

🔷 Real-World Examples

📦 E-commerce: Basic packaging → Premium brand design💻 SaaS: Overwhelming UI → Streamlined UX with onboarding🤖 AI Tools: Complicated workflows → Simple, templated results🚚 Local Services: Unreliable movers → Tech-enabled premium experience🎓 Online Education: Overload of content → Guided curriculum with community + coaching
