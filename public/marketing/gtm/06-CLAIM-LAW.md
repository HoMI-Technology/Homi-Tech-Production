# 6 · Guard claim law harder as growth pressure rises

## Principle
Growth pressure makes founders cut corners on language. **That is when HōMI dies.**  
Claim law tightens under pressure — it never loosens.

## Never-say (absolute)
| Never | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| Approved / pre-qualified / you qualify | Readiness signal / educational exploration |
| Guaranteed / risk-free | (remove) |
| Replaces your credit score | Answers a different question: readiness |
| Our lenders / best deal / unlock dream home | (forbidden) |
| Don’t wait — prices are rising | Consider whether timing supports this decision |
| This is financial advice | Educational guidance only |
| Bank-level / military-grade security | We take privacy and security seriously |
| HōMI Score | Decision Readiness Score |
| HōMI Compass | Threshold Compass |

Full table also in: `NEVER_SAY.md` (this folder).

## Pre-publish ritual (every post, email, ad, PH blurb)
1. Read once for never-say hits.  
2. Would a reasonable person think we **underwrite credit**? → rewrite.  
3. Any **number** (save $, days to home, % ready)? → source file or delete.  
4. Testimonials: process &gt; miracle; no fake/incentivized 5-stars.  
5. Founder OK on pocketbook claims.

## Where claim law lives in product
- Assessment / results surfaces  
- Marketing pages  
- Companion chat (no personalized “you should buy”)  
- Support macros  

## Growth-pressure scenarios
| Pressure | Wrong response | Right response |
|----------|----------------|----------------|
| Slow signups | “Get approved today” ads | Better activation + clearer problem content |
| PH competition | Hype guarantees | Demo + honesty + reply quality |
| Partner asks for co-branded loans | Affiliate deal | Decline; zero-affiliate doctrine |

## Legal note
Not legal advice. U.S. FTC cares about net impression of money claims. “Not financial advice” in a footer does not fix a deceptive hero.
