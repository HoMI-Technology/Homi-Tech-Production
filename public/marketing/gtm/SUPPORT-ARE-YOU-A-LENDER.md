# Support script — “Are you a lender?”

**When:** DMs, email, PH comments, support, LinkedIn comments.  
**Goal:** Close the misread in one breath; never sell harder under pressure.

---

## Short reply (copy-paste)

> No — HōMI is not a lender, broker, credit bureau, or financial advisor.  
> We’re a **Decision Companion**: educational guidance on whether you’re *ready* for a commitment (money + emotion + timing), not whether a bank would approve you.  
> Afford ≠ ready. We never pre-approve, pre-qualify, or replace a credit score.

---

## Medium reply (email / longer DM)

> Thanks for asking — that distinction matters.  
>  
> **HōMI is not a lender.** We don’t underwrite, approve, or shop mortgages. We don’t take referral fees from lenders (zero-affiliate).  
>  
> What we *do*: give you an honest **Decision Readiness** read — Financial Reality, Emotional Truth, and Perfect Timing — so you can decide if you’ll be okay after the commitment, not just whether someone might fund it.  
>  
> If you’re shopping rates or need a pre-approval letter, talk to a licensed lender. If you want clarity before that pressure, start here: https://homitechnology.com/assessment

---

## If they push “but can you get me approved?”

> We can’t approve anyone — and we never will. Approval is a lender’s job. Our job is the question before that: *will you be okay if you take this on?*  
> Not yet / Build First are protection, not a grade.
