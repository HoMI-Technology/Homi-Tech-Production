# Safe Zones

## X header (1500×500)

- Circular avatar sits **bottom-left** (~200–220px).
- Keep logo/type **right of x≈280** and roughly vertical center.
- Leave ~40–60px top/bottom for mobile crop.

## LinkedIn personal (1584×396)

- Profile photo overlaps **bottom-left**.
- Keep critical type center-left / center; avoid far edges.

## LinkedIn Page (4200×700)

- Multi-device crop. Prefer center band; avoid lower-right clutter.

## Facebook cover (851×315)

- Profile photo overlaps **left**.
- Start wordmark around x≥260–280.

## YouTube (2560×1440)

- **All logos and type** inside center **1546×423**.
- Outer regions: atmosphere / compass décor only.

## Stories (1080×1920)

- Keep CTAs and wordmark away from top ~250px and bottom ~300px UI chrome.
