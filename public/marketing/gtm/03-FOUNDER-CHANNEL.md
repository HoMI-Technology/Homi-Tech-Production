# 3 · One founder channel · 90 days

## Principle
Solo founders die of **channel sprawl**. Pick **one** public surface for 90 days. Use the kit as fuel — do not invent a new visual system weekly.

## Decision (fill)
**Primary channel this quarter:** [ ] LinkedIn founder  [ ] X founder  [ ] Other: ________

## Cadence (default)
| Frequency | What |
|-----------|------|
| 3–5× / week | Founder posts (problem / pillars / not-yet / product truth) |
| Daily | 10 meaningful comments on ICP posts (links only when earned) |
| Weekly | 1 longer piece or carousel |

## 90-day rule
Do **not** add Instagram/TikTok/YouTube as primary until:
- Primary channel produced activations, **or**  
- 90 days elapsed and kill-criteria say switch.

## Hours
Recommend **5–7 hrs/week** on this pillar inside a 10–12 hr GTM week.
