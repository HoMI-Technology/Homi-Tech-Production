# 4 · Teach “afford ≠ ready” (without becoming a lender)

## Principle
HōMI’s category entry is a **question**, not a loan product.  
Growth content must teach the problem without sliding into underwriting language.

## Core teaching stack
1. **Afford ≠ ready** — spreadsheet can say yes while life says wait.  
2. **Three lenses** — Financial Reality · Emotional Truth · Perfect Timing.  
3. **Not yet is not no** — protection, not shame.  
4. **Build First is the map** — next action, not a grade.  
5. **What we are not** — not lender, not credit replacement, not advice.

## Safe voice examples
| Say | Don’t say |
|-----|-----------|
| Will you be okay after the commitment? | You’re approved to buy |
| Educational guidance only | This is financial advice |
| Readiness signal | Pre-qualified |
| Talk to a qualified professional when needed | Skip the advisor — our AI knows |

## SEO / long-form topics (hub)
- Afford vs ready  
- Am I ready checklist (self-assessment framework)  
- Should I wait (decision framework, not market prophecy)  
- Rent vs buy (assumptions labeled)  
- Before you talk to a lender (prep, not pre-approval)

## Success metric
Shares/saves of **problem language**, and inbound that says “this is what I’ve been feeling” — not “can you get me a mortgage?”
