# 7 · Run the OS weekly (not once)

## Principle
Documents don’t create growth. **Rituals** do.

## Sunday ritual (15–20 min)
1. Open [`WEEKLY-SCOREBOARD.md`](./WEEKLY-SCOREBOARD.md)  
2. Fill metrics from `/admin/marketing` + email tool  
3. Note source of last 10 activations  
4. Pick **one** experiment for next week  
5. Pre-pick 3 posts from `/marketing/content/` + captions  

## Weekly time budget (default 10–12 hrs)

| Block | Hours | Pillar |
|------:|------:|--------|
| Metrics + plan | 1.5 | 1, 7 |
| Create / adapt content | 4 | 3, 4 |
| Distribute + engage | 2.5 | 3, 4 |
| User conversations | 2 | 1 |
| Email / list | 1–1.5 | 2 |
| Claim check | 0.5 | 6 |

## Weekly checklist
- [ ] Activations counted (not just signups)  
- [ ] Email list grew or engagement measured  
- [ ] Primary founder channel: ≥3 posts  
- [ ] At least one “afford ≠ ready” / pillars / not-yet teaching piece  
- [ ] No amplifier (PH/press) unless engine gates passed  
- [ ] Zero claim-law violations  
- [ ] Scoreboard updated  

## Monthly
- Re-read master OS: `HOMI-SOLO-GTM-OS.md`  
- Kill one channel or experiment that produced no activations  
- Re-copy any Desktop kit work **into** `public/marketing/` (SoT)

## Rule
If you skip the weekly OS two weeks in a row, **do not** schedule a launch spike.
