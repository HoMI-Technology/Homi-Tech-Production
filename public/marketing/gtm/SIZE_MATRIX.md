## Brand rules

- **Avatar** = Threshold Compass only (no wordmark).
- **Covers / OG / posts** = wordmark HōMI (ō macron) + optional tagline + compass accent.
- Spelling: **HōMI** only. Colors: H cyan · ō emerald · M gold · I cyan.
- Field: navy `#0a1628`. Never-say list applies to all copy in pixels and captions.
