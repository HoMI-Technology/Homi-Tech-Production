# 2 · Own an engaged email list

## Principle
Algorithms are rented. **Email is owned land.**

## Targets (planning, not promises)
| Stage | Engaged emails | Meaning of “engaged” |
|-------|----------------|----------------------|
| Soft launch | 100–300 | Opened or clicked in last 30 days, or replied |
| Competitive PH | ~400–1,500 | Same + willing to show up launch morning |
| Quality over size | — | 300 warm > 5,000 cold |

## Weekly actions
- Grow list via value content + personal outreach (e.g. 10 ICP touches/day early).  
- Prefer **click rate** over open rate as engagement signal.  
- Never buy fake list density.
