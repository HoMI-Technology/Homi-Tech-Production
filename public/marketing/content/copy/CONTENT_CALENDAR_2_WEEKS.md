## Weekly rhythm (simple)

1. **Teach** (quote or tip)  
2. **Deepen** (one pillar)  
3. **Humanize** (not yet / build first)  
4. **Clarify product** (what HōMI is / isn’t)  

Always educational. Never pressure “buy now.”
