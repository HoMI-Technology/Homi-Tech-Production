### What is HōMI?
HōMI is a **Decision Companion** for **Decision Readiness Intelligence™**. It helps you explore whether you will be okay after a financial commitment — not whether a lender would approve you.

### What problem does it solve?
Most tools optimize for access (“can you get it?”). People still feel pressure, shame, and timing risk. HōMI focuses on readiness: numbers, emotional truth, and timing — with anti-shame framing.

### How does it work?
You explore readiness through three lenses:
1. **Financial Reality**  
2. **Emotional Truth**  
3. **Perfect Timing**  

Outputs are educational. They are not bank decisions.

### Is HōMI a credit score?
**No.** HōMI answers a different question: readiness. It does not replace credit scores or credit bureaus.

### Is HōMI a lender or mortgage broker?
**No.** HōMI is not a lender, broker, realtor, RIA, or product marketplace. There is no “our lenders” funnel.

### Is this financial advice?
**No.** HōMI provides **educational guidance only**. It is not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice. Consult qualified professionals when you need them.

### Will HōMI tell me I’m “approved”?
**No.** We do not use approval / pre-qualification language. Favorable readiness is not a lending decision.

### What does “Build First” or “Not yet” mean?
They mean clarity and protection — not personal failure. Build First is a map of next constructive steps.

### Who is it for?
People facing meaningful financial decisions who want calm clarity before a hard-to-undo commitment. Home is a wedge; the vision is readiness for financial decisions more broadly.

### Who is it not for?
Anyone seeking a loan offer, rate shopping from HōMI, or a guarantee. We don’t do that.
