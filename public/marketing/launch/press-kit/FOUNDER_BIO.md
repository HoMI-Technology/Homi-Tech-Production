## Short (50 words)

Chike “Wally” Wali is the founder of HōMI (HOMI TECHNOLOGIES LLC). He is building Decision Readiness Intelligence — a Decision Companion that asks whether you’ll be okay after a commitment, not whether you can get approved. Educational guidance only.

## Medium (120 words)

Chike “Wally” Wali is the founder of HōMI, a Decision Readiness Intelligence platform. HōMI helps people explore readiness across Financial Reality, Emotional Truth, and Perfect Timing before financial commitments they can’t easily undo.

Wally’s work focuses on clarity without pressure: anti-shame language, educational guidance, and a hard wall against lender/affiliate incentives. HōMI is not a credit score, not a lender, and not financial advice — it is the readiness layer those systems never provided.
