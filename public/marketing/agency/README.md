## How to run it

1. Open [`/admin/marketing`](/admin/marketing). You are CEO of the ten desks. Do not invent an eleventh.
2. Read [`01-NARRATIVE.md`](./01-NARRATIVE.md) once. Every post, email, and asset must pass the one-liner and the three proof points.
3. Staff the week from [`02-DESKS.md`](./02-DESKS.md). Each desk has a weekly job, a launch-week job, and a definition of done.
4. Execute the calendar in [`03-LAUNCH-90.md`](./03-LAUNCH-90.md). Reuse [`../content/`](../content/) and [`../gtm/`](../gtm/) — do not remake graphics.
5. Ship only lines from [`04-COPY-SYSTEM.md`](./04-COPY-SYSTEM.md) (or claim-checked rewrites of them).
6. Make new pixels only when [`05-CREATIVE-BRIEF.md`](./05-CREATIVE-BRIEF.md) says “make.” Otherwise reuse [`../brand/`](../brand/).
7. Score Sunday from [`06-MEASUREMENT.md`](./06-MEASUREMENT.md) + the live funnel on `/admin/marketing`.

Launch week is a **coordinated burst**: email + LinkedIn + site + UTM. Product Hunt and press are **amplifiers after** an engaged list and stable activation. Do not cancel that gate. Do not make Product Hunt the launch.
