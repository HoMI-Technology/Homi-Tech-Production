## Thesis

Most tools in the home-commitment lane answer **how** — how to shop a rate, how to assemble a packet, how to look fundable. HōMI answers **if**.

The person we serve can often afford the payment on a spreadsheet and still cannot tell whether life after the signature will hold. That gap is not a credit-score problem. It is a readiness problem: money, feeling, and timing in one read.

HōMI is the Decision Companion for that gap. Educational guidance only. The score is recomputed server-side from what the person actually entered. We do not underwrite, place loans, or take a cut from anyone who does.

**Print (use this, not a hotter rewrite):**

> HōMI is the Decision Companion. Decision Readiness Intelligence™. Everyone else tells you how. HōMI tells you if.

## Enemy

Two markets we refuse to join:

### 1. Approval theater
Letters, portals, and ads that treat “someone might fund this” as the finish line. The person still does not know if they will be okay after. We do not compete by sounding more fundable. We name the missing question.

### 2. Afford-only tools
Payment calculators, DTI widgets, and “can you carry this” spreadsheets. Useful. Incomplete. Afford is one input. Ready is three lenses: Financial Reality, Emotional Truth, Perfect Timing.

**We do not punch down on credit scores.** Credit does a real job. It was never designed to answer readiness. That is the opening — not a replacement claim.

**Anti-ICP (do not write for them):** rate shoppers, people hunting a yes from a lender, people who want HōMI to replace a credit score.

## Three true proof points

These are true because the product does them. They are not TAM, regret percentages, or invented “most people” stats. Do not bolt a number onto them.

### 1. Afford ≠ ready
The path scores **Financial Reality (35%) · Emotional Truth (35%) · Perfect Timing (30%)**. A clean payment picture can still land short of ready. Teaching asset: [`/guides/afford-is-not-ready`](/guides/afford-is-not-ready) and [`../content/carousels/afford-vs-ready/`](../content/carousels/afford-vs-ready/).

### 2. Four honest verdicts
The read is not a binary yes/no from a counter. It is one of four:

| Verdict | What it means in public language |
|---------|----------------------------------|
| **READY** | The three lenses align. Still educational — not a funding decision. |
| **ALMOST THERE** | Close. The gaps are specific. |
| **BUILD FIRST** | The map, not a shame grade. |
| **DO NOT PROCEED** | Protection. Not yet is not no. |

Badge label is **DO NOT PROCEED** (never a formal “NOT YET” chip). Weights and thresholds are frozen in product code. Marketing does not invent a fifth verdict or a friendlier rename.

### 3. Educational / zero affiliate
HōMI is not a lender, broker, RIA, credit bureau, realtor, or bank. We do not take referral fees from lenders. Public bright line: [`/guides/what-homi-is-not`](/guides/what-homi-is-not). Support script when someone misreads us: [`../gtm/SUPPORT-ARE-YOU-A-LENDER.md`](../gtm/SUPPORT-ARE-YOU-A-LENDER.md).

## Feeling we are translating (do not dump this into a subject line)

Internally: a readiness operating system for the moment before an undoable commitment.  
Publicly: calm, clear, anti-shame. Curiosity CTAs (“see if”, “explore your readiness”) — never urgency CTAs (“don’t wait”, “prices are rising”).

**Fire, shipped:** the person who can afford it and still cannot sleep gets a companion that will tell them the truth — including wait.

## Narrative tests (Strategy desk, before any campaign)

1. Would a reasonable person think we underwrite or place credit? If yes → rewrite.
2. Does the piece teach afford ≠ ready, name a verdict honestly, or draw a bright line? If none → it is decoration. Kill it.
3. Any statistic? Source file in-repo or delete. No regret %, no invented market size, no “most buyers.”
4. Founder is the byline. No personal-name debate in public copy.
