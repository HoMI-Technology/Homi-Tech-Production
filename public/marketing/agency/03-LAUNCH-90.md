# 03 · Launch 90 — weeks 0–12

**Owner:** Strategy desk · **Operators:** all ten desks ([`02-DESKS.md`](./02-DESKS.md))  
**Engine lock:** LinkedIn founder + owned email · ~10–12 hrs/week  
**North star:** weekly activated users (completed readiness path)

This is a **new** 90-day runbook. It points at assets that already live in `public/marketing/content/` and `public/marketing/gtm/`. Do not remake them. Do not import old wave PDFs.

```text
Weeks 0–2   PRE-LAUNCH     setup + two engine weeks (no burst, no PH)
Weeks 3–4   LAUNCH BURST   email + LinkedIn + site + UTM  (PH stays gated)
Weeks 5–8   ENGINE         weekly OS; teach afford ≠ ready; grow engaged list
Weeks 9–12  AMPLIFIERS     PH / press only if gates pass; else keep engine
```

**Launch week is the burst. Product Hunt is not the launch.** The PH gate in [`../gtm/05-AMPLIFIERS.md`](../gtm/05-AMPLIFIERS.md) stays. Do not cancel it. Do not drag PH into week 3 to “make it feel big.”

Every public link uses UTM. Build them on [`/admin/marketing`](/admin/marketing).

```
https://homitechnology.com/assessment?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=YYYYMMDD_slug
https://homitechnology.com/assessment?utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=YYYYMMDD_slug
```

## Kill rules (any week)

| If… | Then… |
|-----|--------|
| 0 ICP conversations in 7 days | Rewrite comments / offer. Do not add a channel. |
| “Are you a lender?” spikes | Pause promos. Audit last 5 posts. Fix copy. |
| Any claim-law miss | Edit or delete the same day. Log on the scoreboard. |
| Two Sundays skipped | Do not schedule a burst or an amplifier. |
| Burst week with a broken `/assessment` | Stop the remaining posts. Fix the path. |

## What you are not doing in these 90 days

- Importing iCloud / Drive / old wave HTML kits or stale price cards (inventories are research only)  
- Building a second visual system  
- Making Product Hunt the definition of launch  
- Publishing invented TAM, regret %, “73%,” 50/30/20 scoring, “twelve minutes,” or $5 / $500K hooks  
- Signing a personal name — byline is **Founder** only  
- Staffing desks that do not exist
